Our foundation

Three pillars, one calling

Every course of study at IBI is shaped around the same conviction carried in our seal: that God forms the whole person — not the intellect alone. We focus on the importance of God's Word, not the ideas of men, with emphasis on the Word, the Holy Spirit, the anointing, and prayer.

Spiritual formation grounded in Scripture, prayer, and the anointing of the Holy Spirit — the Most Important Presence in every classroom, and the center from which ministry flows.

Faith at work

Who we are

A community formed for kingdom impact

International Bible Institute was founded in January 2013 by Pastor Girma Metaferia, beginning with a small class of 10–15 students meeting part-time in Bole, Addis Ababa. Today IBI serves students across Addis Ababa, Assela, Lege Tafo, Dukem, Bekoji, Bale Robe, and Boditi-Wolayita — in English, Amharic, and Afan Oromiffa.

Our faculty combine academic training with years of pastoral experience, and our graduates return to their churches ready to teach, shepherd, and lead — proficient in the biblical principles of the Kingdom of God (Exodus 19:6).

Institutional partnership

Affiliated with Kingdom Bible Seminary

International Bible Institute is affiliated with Kingdom Bible Seminary (KBS), a seminary whose motto is "Equipping Leaders for Kingdom Impact" and which provides biblical, theological, and leadership education to churches, ministries, and organizations. This affiliation supports IBI's program delivery through approved curriculum and ongoing academic cooperation between the two institutions.

Students benefit from a shared commitment to sound doctrine, practical ministry formation, and responsible, accountable theological education.
