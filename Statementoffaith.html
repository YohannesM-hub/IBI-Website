What we believe

This doctrinal statement reflects the firm belief and stand of International Bible Institute, based on the doctrinal truths of the Bible, and forms the foundation for every course we teach.

God

We believe there is one true God, eternally existent in three persons — God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit — the Creator of heaven and earth and the Redeemer of mankind.

The Word of God

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, the source and sustainer of all things, and the foundation on which we place our faith and trust.

The Condition of Man

We believe people were created good and upright, but through voluntary transgression fell, incurring separation from God with the outcome of spiritual and physical death — and so stand in need of salvation.

Salvation Through Jesus Christ

We believe God's love for mankind was revealed in His Son, Jesus Christ, whose shed blood at Calvary made Him the perfect sacrifice for sin. Man is saved and accepted by God not by works, but by believing and confessing Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord.

Jesus Christ the Lord

We believe in the virgin birth of Jesus, the mediator between God and men; His substitutionary work on the cross; His bodily resurrection; His Lordship and exaltation to the right hand of God; and His personal, future return to reign in power and glory.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit as a gift given by Jesus to the believer subsequent to salvation. The Holy Spirit helps us know God better and empowers the believer for purposeful living and effective ministry.

The Church

We believe in the unity of the Church of Jesus Christ, the body of which He is the head — the fellowship of believers, and the agent through which God's Kingdom purposes are carried out on earth, in the order and structure God intended.

Divine Call

We believe in the priesthood call of every born-again believer, and in a divinely called, spiritually ordained ministry of servant leaders — apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers — to edify and equip believers for service, including water baptism and Holy Communion.

The Blessing of the Lord

We believe God's purpose for mankind is to be blessed and to reign in life — spirit, soul, and body; to be fruitful, to multiply, and to have dominion on the earth. We believe in divine healing, and that God's people are agents through which His Kingdom is established on earth.

The Second Coming

We believe in the blessed hope of the literal return of Christ in glory, at a time known only to the Father; in the resurrection of both the living and the dead; and in the final judgment, where those in Christ will rule and reign with Him forever.

“Ye also, as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house, an holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God by Jesus Christ.”
1 Peter 2:5
