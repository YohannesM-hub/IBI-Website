What guides everything we teach

Our vision, mission, and values are not slogans on a wall — they shape every course we design and every student we send out, in response to the Great Commission.

Vision

A trusted, global benchmark

Through our mission activities, IBI will be trusted by the church worldwide as a leading authority for holistic transformation into a royal priesthood, and will be the global benchmark for excellence in discipleship and leadership development.

Mission

Empowering a holy priesthood

In response to the Great Commission, International Bible Institute exists to empower people with knowledge and understanding of the Word of God — teaching concepts, values, and skills so they can practically apply Biblical principles of the Kingdom of God (Exodus 19:6).

Statement of purpose

Producing servant leaders

IBI exists to produce competent, ethical, and servant leaders — training people without distinction of ethnic, social, economic, or political background, so the whole community can be equipped and served.

What we hold to

Core values & school philosophies

  • Christ and His Church, above every other allegiance
  • Community transformation through biblical studies and a reading culture
  • Teamwork that ensures equal opportunity for every student
  • Stewardship of resources, time, and calling
  • Integrity and excellence in all instruction and administration
  • Scripture as our final authority in every matter of faith and life

Our objectives

What we aim to accomplish

  • Provide sound biblical and theological education at every level, from Certificate to Bachelor of Arts
  • Form students spiritually through discipleship, prayer, and mentorship — not academics alone
  • Equip graduates with practical ministry skills through supervised local-church service
  • Serve the wider church in Addis Ababa and beyond through seminars, resources, and partnership
  • Maintain high standards of academic integrity and institutional accountability
