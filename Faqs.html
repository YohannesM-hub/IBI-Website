Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from prospective students and their families.

Admissions

Applying to IBI

What documents do I need to apply?

A completed application form, two passport-size photographs, a CV, a church recommendation, and — for English-medium students — a passing score on the IBI English Entrance Exam.

What are the entry requirements for the Diploma program?

An ESLCE score of 1.6 or above, or a 10+2 vocational diploma equivalent to the ESLCE from a recognized, accredited institution, with a cumulative GPA of 50% or a passing grade under that institution's grading system.

What are the entry requirements for the Degree program?

An ESLCE score of 2.0 or higher, or a diploma from a recognized and accredited institution. Diploma credits transfer into the BA program at a 2:1 ratio.

Do I need prior theological education to apply?

No. Our Certificate program welcomes new students, and it's designed for those who don't yet meet Diploma or Degree entry requirements.

Can I transfer credits from another institution?

Yes. You'll need to obtain an official transcript from your previous institution and submit it when applying. If admitted without a transcript in hand, you have one year to provide it — after that, transfer credits can't be accepted. IBI determines which credits transfer, and no transfer credits are accepted during a student's final 34 credits.

Studying at IBI

Programs & scheduling

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes. Day classes run Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–1:00 PM, and evening classes run Monday–Saturday, 5:30 PM–7:30 PM — built for students already serving in ministry or employed full-time.

What languages are courses taught in?

English, Amharic, and Afan Oromiffa, depending on program and campus.

How long do the programs take?

The Certificate track (36 credit hours) runs one year, the Diploma track (72 credit hours) runs two years, and the Bachelor of Arts (128 credit hours) runs four years — each with part-time pacing available.

What does "educating the whole man" mean in practice?

Every program combines classroom study (mind) with spiritual formation through prayer and discipleship (spirit), and hands-on ministry practice in local churches (body).

What happens if I fail a course?

A grade of 70% or above is required to pass. Courses graded below that mark must be retaken.

Fees & support

Costs & assistance

What fees should I expect?

Tuition is billed per term and is due in full at registration — students pay 100% of the first term's tuition at the time they register. Fees do not include textbook rental or study guide purchases. Application fees are non-refundable, but tuition is refundable on a pro-rata basis if a student must leave during a course.

Is financial assistance available?

Speak with our admissions office about your circumstances — each student's educational expenses are their own responsibility, but we work individually with students where support is available.

Who do I contact with more questions?

Our admissions office is glad to help — reach out through the contact page and we'll respond promptly.

Still have a question?

Our admissions office is happy to help you find the right program and next steps.
