How we teach

Classroom instruction, mentorship, and field practice

Learning at IBI happens in three places at once: in the classroom, where Scripture and doctrine are taught with care; in mentorship, where faculty walk alongside each student; and in the field, where students serve in local churches as part of their formation.

The academic year runs from September to June across three terms of three months, with a fifteen-day break between terms. Day classes meet Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–1:00 PM; evening classes meet Monday–Saturday, 5:30 PM–7:30 PM — built for students already serving in ministry or working full-time.
